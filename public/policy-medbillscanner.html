Privacy Policy

Med Bill Scanner · Last updated: 18 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Med Bill Scanner (the “App”) handles your information. The App helps you scan and understand Swiss medical bills. It was built to keep your data on your device: your bills are processed locally and are not stored on, or transmitted to, any server operated by us.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller responsible for processing in connection with the App is:

  • Dominik Lüchinger
  • Switzerland
  • Contact: domi.luechinger@gmail.com

2. Data processed on your device

The following are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us:

  • Document images. When you scan a bill with the camera or import a file/photo, the image is used to recognise text. Text recognition (OCR) runs locally using Google’s on-device ML Kit. Images are not sent to us or to any cloud OCR service.
  • Bill content. Amounts, dates, tariff codes and other details extracted from your bill are held only in the App’s memory while you view them. They are not written to any server we operate.

3. Data stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device (using the operating system’s standard local storage). This never leaves your device:

  • Your selected language
  • Your selected appearance (light / dark / system theme)

4. Network connections

The App makes a limited number of outbound connections:

  • Medication / article lookup. When a tariff or medication code on your bill is not contained in the catalogues bundled with the App, the App may send only that code (for example a medication article number) to the Swiss reference database index.refdata.ch to retrieve the corresponding product name. No personal data, no images, and no other bill content are sent. This lookup is governed by refdata’s own privacy practices.
  • External links. The App contains links to official Swiss information sources (e.g. tarmed-browser.ch, tardoc.ch, bag.admin.ch) and to this policy. Opening a link launches your browser and is subject to the privacy policy of the destination site.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.
  • We do not use crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.

6. Device permissions

  • Camera — used only to scan documents you choose to scan.
  • Photos / files — used only to import a document you select.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time in your device settings.

7. Legal basis and your rights

Because the App processes your data locally on your device and we do not collect personal data on our servers, we generally do not hold personal data about you. Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP/nDSG) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights including access, rectification, erasure, and objection. As your bill data lives only on your device, you can exercise control directly: deleting the data, or uninstalling the App, removes it. For any questions about this policy, contact us at the address in section 1.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

10. Disclaimer on accuracy

The App extracts figures from your bills automatically and they may contain errors. The App is an aid and does not constitute medical, legal, insurance, or financial advice. Always verify amounts against your original documents.
